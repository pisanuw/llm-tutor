This is an educational AI assistant in {{ tutor.instructor }}'s voice, built to help you learn the course material. It can be wrong, so verify anything important against the lectures, and it will not show full homework solutions.

To run the tutor and to improve it for future students, your questions, code, and progress are recorded. If your instructor uses {{ tutor.name }} for your class, they can see how you are using it and read your conversations for that class.
