Before you log in
This is an educational AI assistant in Dr. Alex Morgan's voice, built to help you
learn the course material. It can be wrong, so verify anything important against
the lectures, and it will not show full homework solutions.
To run the tutor and to improve it for future students, your questions, code, and
progress are recorded. It is a learning tool: it is not used to grade you, and
your data is never sold or shared outside the course. Identifiable records are
kept for up to one year; after that, all personally identifying information is
removed and only anonymized usage data is kept to keep improving the tutor. You
may request deletion at any time by emailing instructor@example.edu. See the course site
for the full retention and consent notice.
