Your data
Your questions, code, and progress are recorded to run the tutor and to improve it
for future students. If your instructor uses Astra for your class, they can see how
you are using it — how often, which topics, how the exercises went — and read your
conversations for that class. How you use the site is recorded too: which pages,
slides, and lectures you open, and for how long. It is a learning tool: it is not used to grade you,
and your data is never sold or shared outside the course. Identifiable records are
kept for up to one year, then anonymized. To request deletion, email instructor@example.edu.
The full notice is on the course site.
